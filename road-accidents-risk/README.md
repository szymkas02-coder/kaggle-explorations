# Road Accidents Risk

The dataset is from a Kaggle competition on predicting road accident risk, containing features such as road conditions, weather, and traffic factors. The project includes EDA to understand accident patterns and risk factors, followed by simple classification models to predict accident severity or probability.

Key finding: EDA highlighted that certain environmental and infrastructure features were the strongest predictors of elevated accident risk.

This is a learning exercise completed during early ML exploration — not a production-ready solution.
